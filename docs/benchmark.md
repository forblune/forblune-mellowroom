# Benchmark brief

Research date: 2026-08-13

This concept was shaped after reviewing recurring patterns on the following public sites:

- https://www.sageflowstudio.co.uk/
- https://www.maccs.digital/industries/health-wellness
- https://www.sqspagency.com/squarespace-website-design-for-health-wellness
- https://www.eukistudio.com/

The references informed only high-level patterns: warm and calm presentation, specific service choices, trust details close to the decision, visible booking actions, and mobile-first interaction. Their wording, assets, layouts, identities, testimonials, and claims were not copied.

Forblune's differentiation is a working bilingual booking flow plus an inspectable owner-operations view, with an explicit self-initiated concept boundary.
